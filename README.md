## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from **UFAZ (Azerbaijan-France University)**, holding dual degrees from **Azerbaijan State Oil and Industry University (ASOIU)** and the **University of Strasbourg**, graduating with honors.

I'm passionate about technology and enjoy exploring different areas of software engineering, from building mobile applications to developing intelligent systems.

My professional focus is **Mobile Development**, particularly Android and Kotlin Multiplatform, while my growing passion lies in **Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science**.

I love understanding how things work, experimenting with new technologies, and turning ideas into real-world applications.

### 🚀 What I'm passionate about

- 📱 **Mobile Development:** Building modern, scalable Android and cross-platform applications.
- 🤖 **Machine Learning:** Exploring algorithms, predictive models, and intelligent systems.
- 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence:** Understanding how AI can solve real-world problems.
- 📊 **Data Science:** Data analysis, visualization, feature engineering, and statistical learning.
- 🏗️ **Software Engineering:** Clean Architecture, design patterns, and maintainable code.
- 🌱 **Continuous Learning:** Always curious about emerging technologies and new challenges.
